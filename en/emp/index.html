Microprocesadores Empotrados

Writing embedded control programs with POSIX on Linux-powered ARM boards (Raspberry Pi). Four Temas arranged into four chapters: understand the core distinction between "microprocessor + OS" and microcontrollers, then progressively build multitasking, signals & POSIX timers, and network communications. Each chapter starts with a guiding question, develops mechanisms step-by-step through animations, and concludes with key takeaways, self-tests, and randomized numerical drills.
